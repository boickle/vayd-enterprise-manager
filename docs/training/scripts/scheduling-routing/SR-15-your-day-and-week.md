# SR-15. Your day and week on the calendar

- **Audience:** doctors and techs.
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** SR-01.
- **The one thing to remember:** check your day the evening before: the arrival windows, the drive, and anything flagged.

## Why (opening)

"Families plan their day around the window we gave them. Two minutes with your schedule the night before means fewer surprises and fewer late arrivals."

## What else you can do here

- **Month** view for the longer picture.
- **APPOINTMENT TYPE** to show one kind of visit.
- Right-click a card for **On Visit Day** tools (SR-16).

## What other roles see

- **Techs** pick the doctor they're riding with in **PRIMARY PROVIDER**.
- **CLs** see the same calendar for every doctor.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: reading the start time instead of the window, and finding out about a missing Room Loader in the driveway. Remember: check your day the evening before: the arrival windows, the drive, and anything flagged."
