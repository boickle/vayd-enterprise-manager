# INV-17. The most common money mistakes

- **Audience:** everyone.
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** the rest of the series.
- **The one thing to remember:** charge once, check the invoice, and refund before you void.

## Why (opening)

"Most money problems at a practice come from a few habits. Here they are, and how to avoid each."
