# PERM-02. Give someone a role, for one office or the whole practice

- **Audience:** managers (practice admins and practice managers).
- **Length:** 2 to 3 minutes.
- **Watch first:** PERM-01.
- **The one thing to remember:** techs, vets and inventory managers get their role per office. Tick the office they work at, because the office decides their stock rights and automatic tasks.

## Why (opening)

"A new hire can't do anything in Scout until they have a role. Give it once, at the right office, and their screens, stock and tasks follow."

## What else you can do here

- Untick a person and **Save assignees** to remove a role.
- **Show deactivated (N)** lists people who have left, labelled "(Deactivated)", so you can take their roles off.
- **Manual booking permissions** on each role: which appointment types that role may book by hand on the calendar.

## What other roles see

- **Practice managers** can give and remove roles too, except the **Practice Admin**, **Practice Manager** and **Medical Director** roles: only a platform superadmin changes those.
- **Nobody can change their own roles.** On your own row the change is refused.
- **Everyone else** has no Roles page.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: ticking a tech under the wrong office, so their stock and tasks go to the wrong place; and forgetting the second office for someone who works at both. Remember: techs, vets and inventory managers get their role per office. Tick the office they work at."
