# LV-04. Finish in IDEXX: specimen questions, order, requisition

- **Audience:** techs and doctors.
- **Length:** 2½ minutes.
- **Watch first:** LV-03.
- **The one thing to remember:** the order isn't at IDEXX until you click **Order** in IDEXX's page and print the requisition.

## Why (opening)

"Scout makes the IDEXX order, but IDEXX asks its own questions. Until you answer them and click Order, the sample has no requisition."

## What else you can do here

- **Open in new tab** opens IDEXX's page in its own tab; **Close** closes the window.
- **Refresh** checks IDEXX again if the window was closed.
- A test removed in IDEXX's page comes off the bill when Scout added it and the bill is open; otherwise Billing gets a task.

## What other roles see

- Same for doctors and techs.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: closing IDEXX's page without clicking Order, and sending the sample without the requisition. Remember: the order isn't at IDEXX until you click Order in IDEXX's page and print the requisition."
