# MEM-13. Change a pet's plan

- **Audience:** lead CLs and managers (any time); doctors, techs and CLs in the first month of the membership year.
  Filmed as Casey Morgan (client liaison), within the first month.
- **Length:** about 1¼ minutes (at 1.25×).
- **Watch first:** MEM-10.
- **The one thing to remember:** after the first month of the membership year, only a lead client liaison or a
  manager can change the plan.

## Why (opening)

"Sometimes a pet starts on the wrong plan, or the family wants a different one. Change plan moves the membership, and its billing, in one step."

## What else you can do here

- **Keep staff-added benefits** keeps benefits added for this pet only (MEM-11).
- Moving to a pricier plan charges the difference for the year so far now; moving to a cheaper one credits it to the
  next bills (owner decision, Oct 29, 2026). Monthly ↔ annual uses Stripe's own proration.
- For a member still billed through Square, the dialog gives the exact amount to charge or refund in Square by hand.

## What other roles see

- **After the first month**, doctors, techs and CLs see **Change plan** greyed out, with the hover note "More than a
  month into this membership year. Ask a lead client liaison, practice manager or admin to change the plan."
- Lead CLs and managers can change it any time.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: unticking Carry usage over, and cancelling and re-enrolling instead, which starts a new year and a second charge. Remember: after the first month of the membership year, only a lead client liaison or a manager can change the plan."
