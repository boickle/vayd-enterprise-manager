## What other roles see

- Practice managers and admins can edit plans and archive them. Only admins can apply changes to existing members.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: a plan with no renewal or age-out plan, and pushing a change to existing members by accident. Remember: a plan change affects new members only, unless an admin pushes it to existing members on purpose."
