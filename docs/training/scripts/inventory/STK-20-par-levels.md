# STK-20. Par levels

- **Audience:** practice manager.
- **Length:** 2 to 3 minutes.
- **Watch first:** STK-07, STK-18.
- **The one thing to remember:** par sets what each car is refilled to, and Main's re-order point and max set what gets ordered.

## Why (opening)

"The Transfer List and the Order List are only as good as the targets behind them. Too low and cars run out; too high and money sits on the shelf."

## Steps

Signed in as Robin Hale. Dr. Shah's Car 2 should carry 6 Cerenia tablets.

| Step | Screen | Action | Narration | Demo data needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pharmacy | Click **Setup**, then **Par Levels** | "Open Par Levels." | — |
| 2 | Par Levels | Choose **Office**: Raleigh office | "Pick the office." | Pars from the setup |
| 3 | Par Levels | Point at the one thing to remember (voice only) | "Remember: par refills the cars; re-order point and max fill Main." | — |
| 4 | Par Levels | Point at the Cerenia 24 mg tablet block: Main (default) 40, Car 3 "short 7", Car 2 0 | "Each item shows every location: on hand, the target, and whether it's short." | — |
| 5 | Par Levels | Point at **Re-order** and **Max** on Main (default) | "Main has a re-order point and a max." | — |
| 6 | Par Levels | Point at **Max / Par** on Car | "Cars have a par: what they're refilled to." | — |
| 7 | Par Levels | Type `6` in Car 2's par | "Car 2 should carry six." | — |
| 8 | Par Levels | Click **Save** on the Cerenia block | "Save." | — |
| 9 | Par Levels | Point at Car 2 "short 6" | "Car 2 is now short six, so it goes on the Transfer List." | — |

## What else you can do here

- Filter: **Only needs fill or order**, **Transfer list**, **Order list**, **All**.
- **Add item to this office** and **Other stock**.
- **Whole location (bulk)** sets a whole car or Main at once (STK-24).

## What other roles see

- Only the practice manager sees **Setup → Par Levels**.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes are setting par on Main instead of re-order and max, and forgetting a new car. Remember: par refills the cars; re-order point and max fill Main."
