# PRC-09. Archive or restore an item

- **Audience:** practice manager.
- **Length:** 1 minute.
- **Watch first:** PRC-02.
- **The one thing to remember:** archiving hides an item from search; it doesn't delete it or its history.

## Why (opening)

"Items we stop carrying clutter every search. Archive them, and they're out of the way but still on old bills and reports."
