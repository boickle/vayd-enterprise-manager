# MEM-03. Post-appointment sign-up: re-price the visit and refund (owner here, or email the agreement)

- **Audience:** CLs (every visit role can run it). Filmed as Casey Morgan (client liaison).
- **Length:** about 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** MEM-01, MEM-02.
- **The one thing to remember:** sign them up within 24 business hours of the visit, and Scout does the refund for you.
- Includes the old MEM-04 (email the agreement when the owner isn't with you).

## Why (opening)

"Families often decide to join after the visit, once they've seen the bill. If they join within twenty-four business hours, Scout re-prices that visit as if they'd been members, and refunds the difference."

## What else you can do here

- **Annual / Monthly** switches the plan cards to annual prices.
- **Assign to** on a not-covered line counts it against a plan benefit when it's the same service under another
  name (for example an annual wellness visit billed as a senior wellness visit).
- **Don't issue the refund automatically**: the membership is still created and the visit re-priced, but the money
  stays put, for when you'll refund by hand.
- **Load earlier** and **Show all paid invoices** reach older visits. Past the window you need the override (below).
- When the owner has no card on file, a **Card for the membership** box appears for the card that will pay the plan.

## What other roles see

- Every visit role can run it within the window.
- **Past the window:** lead CLs and managers see **Override the post-visit window** with a required reason, which is
  written to the audit trail. Everyone else sees "Ask a manager or lead client liaison to override it."

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: waiting past the window, and refunding by hand on top of this. Remember: sign them up within twenty-four business hours of the visit, and Scout does the refund for you."
