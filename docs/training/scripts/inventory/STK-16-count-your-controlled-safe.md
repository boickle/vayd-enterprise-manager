# STK-16. Count your controlled safe (First / Annual Counts)

- **Audience:** doctors (who manage their own safe) and the practice manager.
- **Length:** 2 to 3 minutes.
- **Watch first:** STK-11.
- **The one thing to remember:** count every bottle in the safe, in mL. Each bottle Scout knows about has its own row; write 0 for one that's gone.

## Why (opening)

"Until a safe is counted, Scout doesn't know what's really in it. The first count sets the starting balance that the Controlled Drugs report works from."

## Steps

Signed in as Dr. Avery Lane. Her Controlled safe has two Buprenorphine bottles: BU-1101 (7.9 mL) and BU-1102 (10 mL). It hasn't been counted yet.

## What else you can do here

- **Print count sheet** or **Download Excel sheet to fill in**, then **Upload filled sheet…**.
- **Annual count** for the yearly recount.
- **When the count was taken**, if you counted earlier in the day.

## What other roles see

- Doctors see only **Setup → First / Annual Counts**.
- The practice manager sees every office.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes are counting only the open bottle, and guessing the mL. Remember: every bottle, in mL, with its lot."
