# MEM-10. How many exams are left? Reading benefits and the audit trail

- **Audience:** CLs and techs. Filmed as Casey Morgan (client liaison).
- **Length:** about 1¼ minutes.
- **Watch first:** MEM-01.
- **The one thing to remember:** options that share an allowance draw down together. Using one uses them all.

## Why (opening)

"Clients often call to ask what's left on their plan. You can answer in seconds from the pet's chart."
