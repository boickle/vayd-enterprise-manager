> **Dry run:** passed Nov 9, 2026 (`mem-16-owner-changed.cy.ts`). Change: **Mark handled** first asks what you decided (a note for the audit trail); the video types one.

## Why (opening)

"When a pet moves to a new owner, the membership, its card and its renewal emails still belong to the old owner. Scout flags it so nobody is billed by mistake."
