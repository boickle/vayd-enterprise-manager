# SR-10. The Forward booking list: follow-ups sent to the CLs

- **Audience:** CLs.
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** EMR V5b (Schedule follow-ups).
- **The one thing to remember:** every card ends booked, Book later, or removed. Work the oldest and highest priority first.

## Why (opening)

"When a doctor sends a follow-up to the CLs, the pet's next recheck or vaccine depends on us booking it."

## What else you can do here

- **Contact client**, **Email history** and **Messages history** on each card.
- **Edit** changes the target, type or doctor.
- **Include HOLDs** shows follow-ups that are on hold.
- **+ Forward Booking** in the left menu opens the Schedule follow-ups window for a pet or owner (EMR V5b).

## What other roles see

- **Doctors and techs** can create follow-ups and see this list, and doctors only their own.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: booking the visit from the calendar and leaving the card in Needs booking, and calling without logging it. Remember: every card ends booked, Book later, or removed. Work the oldest and highest priority first."

## Notes from the check in the app (Nov 7, 2026)

- **Checked as a CL:** the list, the card and its buttons.
- **Not walked:** **Book** through to the booked state, **Book later…** and **Mark complete…**.
- **Target on a closed day:** a 2-week follow-up created on a Saturday showed "Target: Sat, Nov 21, 2026". Harmless (routing searches around it), but say "around".
- **The note I added** ("Call the cell; gate code 4412") wasn't on the card. Check where household notes show (**Edit**?) in the dry run.
