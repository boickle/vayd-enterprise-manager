# STK-09. Waste / Adjust: expired, broken or lost

- **Audience:** technicians and doctors.
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** STK-01.
- **The one thing to remember:** Waste / Adjust is for stock that left without a sale. A miscount is fixed by a count, not by waste.

## Why (opening)

"Expired, broken or lost stock has to come off the shelf, with an honest reason. That's what makes the shrink numbers mean something."

## What else you can do here

- Other reasons: **Damaged**, **Broken**, **Lost**, **Leakage**, **Other Correction** (needs an explanation).
- **Add lot** if the bottle isn't listed.

## What other roles see

- Doctors waste from their own car the same way.
- Controlled drugs also need a disposal method and can have a witness (STK-10).
- Client liaisons don't see this screen (owner, Nov 9: they don't count or unbox).

## Closing

"The most common mistakes are using waste to fix a miscount, and logging stock that was actually sold. Remember: Waste / Adjust is only for stock that left without a sale."
