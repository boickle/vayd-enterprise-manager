# MEM-08. Covered services during the visit

- **Audience:** doctors and techs. Filmed as Dr. Avery Lane.
- **Length:** about 1½ minutes.
- **Watch first:** MEM-01, INV-01.
- **The one thing to remember:** when Scout asks which benefit to use, pick one. Once a benefit is used up, the item
  is charged at the regular price.

## Why (opening)

"During a member's visit, you add charges the usual way. Scout works out what the plan covers, so the bill is right before checkout."

## What else you can do here

- **Cancel** in the picker cancels adding the charge altogether. Add it again to choose.
- A covered line's price can't be edited at checkout. It can still be removed; removing it doesn't give the
  allowance back (memberships.md rule 84).
- **Reprice** (INV-14) re-applies member prices if the pet joined after the charges were added.
- Copay and percent-off benefits show as a lower price, not **covered**.

## What other roles see

- Everyone who can add charges to a visit sees the same picker. CLs see it when they add charges at checkout.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: typing a price by hand on a covered line, and closing the benefit question, which cancels the charge. Remember: when Scout asks which benefit to use, pick one. Once a benefit is used up, the item is charged at the regular price."
