# STK-07. Restock your car from the Transfer List

- **Audience:** technicians and doctors.
- **Length:** 2 to 3 minutes.
- **Watch first:** STK-01.
- **The one thing to remember:** transfer only what you physically took off the shelf.

## Why (opening)

"After a day of visits your car is short of some things. The Transfer List tells you exactly what, and where to take it from. Recording it keeps Scout's numbers matching your car."

## What else you can do here

- Change **Quantity** in the window when you took fewer than suggested.
- Pick another **From office** or **From location** when Main is short.
- **Location: All locations** shows every car in the office at once.

## What other roles see

- Doctors use it the same way for their own car.
- The practice manager sees it too, from Pharmacy → Move / Order.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes are clicking Transfer for stock you didn't take, and looking at the wrong office. Remember: transfer only what you took."
