# STK-26. The most common inventory mistakes

- **Audience:** everyone.
- **Length:** 2 to 3 minutes.
- **Watch first:** STK-01 to STK-12.
- **The one thing to remember:** if it moved, record it, on the right shelf, from the right bottle.

## Why (opening)

"Most stock problems come from a handful of habits. Here are the ones that cost us the most, and the screen that fixes each."
