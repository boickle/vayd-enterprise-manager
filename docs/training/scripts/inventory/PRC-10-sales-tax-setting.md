# PRC-10. Sales tax setting

- **Audience:** admin logins.
- **Length:** 1 minute.
- **Watch first:** PRC-06.
- **The one thing to remember:** the rate is a percent: 5.5, not 0.055.

## Why (opening)

"One setting drives the tax on every taxable line: visits, the store and the sales tax report."

## Steps

Signed in with an admin login.

| Step | Screen | Action | Narration | Demo data needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Settings | Click **Inventory**, then **Sales Tax** | "Open Sales Tax." | Admin login |
| 2 | Sales tax | Point at "Level 1 is your practice sales tax." | "Level 1 is our sales tax." | — |
| 3 | Sales tax | Point at the one thing to remember (voice only) | "Remember: a percent, 5.5." | — |
| 4 | Sales tax | Point at **Level 1 name**: Sales Tax and **Level 1 rate (%)**: 5.5 | "Sales Tax, 5.5 percent." | Tax row from eVet (5.5%) |
| 5 | Sales tax | Point at **Show tax level 2** and **Show tax level 3** | "Turn on more levels only if we ever need them." | — |
| 6 | Voice-over | — | "Change it only when the state rate changes, then click Save sales tax." | — |

## What else you can do here

- Nothing else on this tab.

## What other roles see

- Only admin logins open the full Settings.

## Closing

"The most common mistake is typing the rate as a fraction. Remember: a percent, 5.5."
