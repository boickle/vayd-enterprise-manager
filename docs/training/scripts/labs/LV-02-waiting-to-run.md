# LV-02. Finish later: the Waiting to run list

- **Audience:** techs.
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** LV-01.
- **The one thing to remember:** before your day ends, open Labs → In-house labs → **Waiting to run** and finish or explain every lab there.

## Why (opening)

"You don't have to finish a lab in the room. You do have to finish it the same day, or it never reaches the chart."

## What else you can do here

- **Chart** on each row opens the pet's chart.
- **Recently resulted** opens any finished result.

## What other roles see

- Client liaisons see the list, but the form is read-only.

## Closing

"The most common mistake: Save, finish later, then forgetting it. Remember: before your day ends, open Waiting to run and finish or explain every lab there."
