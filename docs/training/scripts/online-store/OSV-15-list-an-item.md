# OSV-15. List an item in the store

- **Audience:** managers.
- **Length:** 2 to 3 minutes.
- **Watch first:** none.
- **The one thing to remember:** set prescription items to "Needs doctor approval"; controlled drugs can't be listed.

## Why (opening)

"What families can buy online comes from this page. Getting approval right here is what keeps prescriptions safe."

## Steps

| Step | Screen | Action | Narration | Demo data needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pharmacy → **Store** → **Store items**, Robin | Point at **On the store (4)** and **Removed (115)** | "Everything listed, and everything taken off." | Demo catalog |
| 2 | Store items | Point at **Dental chews**: **Approval** (Approved), **Requires a pet**, **Home page** **Highlight**, **Remove from store** | "Each product: approval, pet, home page, and remove." | — |
| 3 | Store items | Point at the one thing to remember | "Prescription items: Needs doctor approval. Controlled drugs can't be listed." | — |
| 4 | Heartworm & flea chewable | Point at **Approval**: Needs doctor approval | "The heartworm chewable needs a doctor's OK on every order." | — |
| 5 | Dental chews | Point at **Store categories**, **Sale**, **DESCRIPTION** | "Categories, a sale, and the description the family reads." | — |
| 6 | Store items | Click **+ Add store item** | "To add one, start with + Add store item." | — |
| 7 | Catalog → Gabapentin 100 mg capsules → **Online Store** | Try to list it | "A controlled drug is refused." | Gabapentin (controlled) |

## What else you can do here

- **+ Add inventory item**; filters for **Approval default**, **Highlight on home**, **Autoship**, **Category**.
- Pharmacy → Store → **Coupons** (OSV-16) and **Gift cards** (OSV-14).

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: listing a prescription as Approved, and forgetting the pet setting on a medicine. Remember: set prescription items to Needs doctor approval; controlled drugs can't be listed."
