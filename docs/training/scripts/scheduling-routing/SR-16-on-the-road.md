# SR-16. Visit day on the road: the On My Way text and Start / End Visit

- **Audience:** doctors and techs.
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** SR-15.
- **The one thing to remember:** send On My Way when you leave the house before, and record the real start and end times.

## Why (opening)

"An On My Way text means the family has the dog ready and the door open. Real start and end times teach routing how long our visits actually take."

## What else you can do here

- **On Visit Day → Jot Prep** for your notes before the visit.
- **Start Encounter** opens the SOAP or medical note (EMR videos).
- **Inform DVM of death** when a family calls about a pet that has died.

## What other roles see

- **Techs** see the same tools. If no tech is listed on the visit, the text says "No technician is listed on this visit — edit the greeting if needed."
- **Start / End Visit** is greyed out for future visits: "Start / End Visit is not available for future visits."

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: sending On My Way from the depot hours ahead, and never saving the end time. Remember: send On My Way when you leave the house before, and record the real start and end times."

## Notes from the check in the app (Nov 7, 2026)

- **Checked as Dr. Lane:** the On My Way window and send (the text was captured on the training stack, redirected to a test number), and the Start / End Visit window.
- **Not walked:** saving start and end times, and the follow-up step inside Start / End Visit.
- **Off-production button:** the training stack also shows **Send to actual client** (only outside production). Don't click it, and expect it in the video unless the recording is cropped.
- **Minutes away** isn't filled in from the route: a visit two days away showed 2715. Record on the visit day and type the minutes.
- **The message reads "your VAYD team with Vet At Your Door"**, with VAYD twice. See the review, Q7.
- **The line it sends from:** "Sends from the practice backup phone line (…) — this visit has no provider Quo line." Each doctor needs a Quo line for texts to come from their own number.
- **The wording contradicts itself:** "Follow-up is required when you save end time" and "Optional here — checkout asks this while the client is still at the door." See the review.
