# STK-22. Shrink, Count Report and Activity: who did what

- **Audience:** practice manager and the Controlled Substances Director.
- **Length:** 3 minutes.
- **Watch first:** STK-11.
- **The one thing to remember:** shrink is short counts plus logged waste. When a number looks wrong, the Activity log shows who did what and when.

## Why (opening)

"These three reports tell you whether stock is going missing, whether counts can be trusted, and exactly what happened to any item."

## What else you can do here

- **Office** and **Period** on Shrink & Accuracy.
- **Only off counts**, **Only flagged**, **Needs review** on the Count Report.
- **Action** filter and **Exceptions only** on Activity.

## What other roles see

- Doctors see Activity for controlled drugs only.
- Inventory managers see Activity for their own office.
- Technicians don't see these reports.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes are reading one bad week as a trend, and blaming a person before checking Activity. Remember: shrink is short counts plus waste, and Activity shows who did what."
