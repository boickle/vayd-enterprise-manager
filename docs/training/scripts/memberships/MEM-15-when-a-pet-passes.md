# MEM-15. When a pet passes: the euthanasia estimate and death wrap-up

- **Audience:** doctors, techs and CLs (every visit role can run the wrap-up). Filmed as Casey Morgan (CL).
- **Length:** about 1 minute (at 1.25×).
- **Watch first:** MEM-14; EMR V9 and V10 (euthanasia estimate, consent and payment).
- **The one thing to remember:** always accept the wrap-up. If you close it, the membership keeps billing.

## Why (opening)

"When a pet dies, their membership has to stop, along with reminders, refills and future visits. The wrap-up does it all at once."

## What else you can do here

- **At a euthanasia visit:** the euthanasia estimate adds a membership line (a charge if more was used than paid, a
  credit if less), so the family sees one total. After payment, checkout opens this wrap-up and cancels the
  membership with no second charge or refund ("Membership money is already on this invoice").
- **Ending a euthanasia visit from the scheduler** opens the same wrap-up, with money moving.
- **Inactivating the whole household** on the client page does the same for every pet.
- To refund the unused part as an exception, cancel from the pet's Membership panel first (MEM-14), then inactivate.

## What other roles see

- Every visit role sees **Deactivate** and the wrap-up.
