# ROLE-09. What the Controlled Substances Director sees

- **Audience:** managers.
- **Length:** 1 to 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** ROLE-06.
- **The one thing to remember:** the Controlled Substances Director oversees controlled drugs across every office (counts, shrink, the activity log and the Vet Snap export) and nothing clinical by itself.

## Why (opening)

"Controlled drugs carry legal records. One person reviews them across every office, and that's this role."

## Steps

Logged in as **Drew Ellis** (Controlled Substances Director).

| Step | Screen | Action | Narration | Demo data needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Home | Point at the top bar: **Home, Scheduling, Pharmacy, Labs, Tasks**, no Catalog or Settings | "This is Drew, our Controlled Substances Director." | perm-seed.ts |
| 2 | Voice-over | — | "The one thing to remember: this role oversees controlled drugs in every office: counts, shrink, the activity log and the Vet Snap export." | — |
| 3 | Home | Click **Analytics** | "Analytics opens…" | — |
| 4 | Analytics | Point at **Inventory: Shrink & Accuracy, Count Report, Controlled Drugs, Activity** and "Controlled drugs only." | "…to controlled drugs only: shrink and accuracy, counts, the controlled-drug report by doctor, and activity." | Controlled-drug stock and counts (none seeded yet) |
| 5 | Voice-over | — | "The Vet Snap patient and client file for the DEA log also downloads from here." | — |

## What they can't do

- **Clinical work** by this role alone (signing, counting, dispensing). If the director is also a vet, they hold both roles. They can open client and patient records (owner, Nov 10).
- **Settings, Marketing, Catalog.**

## Notes to check before recording

- **No demo data:** the stack has no controlled-drug stock or counts, so the reports are empty. A controlled-drug seed is needed (none exists for this stack).
- Owner, Nov 10: every role, this one included, holds "View client and patient records".

## Closing

"The most common mistake is expecting this role to sign or count by itself. It reviews; techs and vets count and dispense. Remember: the Controlled Substances Director oversees controlled drugs in every office."
