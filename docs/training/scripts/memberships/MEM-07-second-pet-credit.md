# MEM-07. Second pet: the $75 credit

- **Audience:** CLs. Filmed as Casey Morgan (client liaison).
- **Length:** about 1½ minutes.
- **Watch first:** MEM-03 or MEM-06.
- **The one thing to remember:** the credit posts automatically, once for each extra pet, and it's used at checkout.

## Why (opening)

"When a second pet in the household joins, the family gets a one-time seventy-five dollar account credit. Scout posts it for you."

## What else you can do here

- The credit can pay any visit, refill or store order, in part or in full.
- If Scout can't post the credit, it emails the practice: "Add by hand: Membership Multi-Pet — <pet>", with the
  amount. Only then do you add it by hand.
- If a membership is cancelled in the same year, the credit counts as a benefit the family received (MEM-14).

## What other roles see

- Everyone who can take payment sees **Account credit** as a method.

## Closing

"The most common mistake is adding the credit by hand as well, so the family gets it twice. Remember: the credit posts automatically, once for each extra pet, and it's used at checkout."
