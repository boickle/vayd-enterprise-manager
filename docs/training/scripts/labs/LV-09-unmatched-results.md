# LV-09. Unmatched IDEXX results

- **Audience:** techs (lead techs at cutover).
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** LV-06.
- **The one thing to remember:** check **Unmatched** every day, and match each result to the right pet before anything else.

## Why (opening)

"Scout matches most IDEXX results by itself. The ones it can't, like tests ordered in eVet or on IDEXX's website, wait in Unmatched, and no doctor gets a task until they're matched."

## What else you can do here

- **All recent** shows matched and unmatched results together.

## What other roles see

- Client liaisons don't see **Which pet is this?**.

## Closing

"The most common mistake: matching to the first pet with the right name. Check the owner. Remember: check Unmatched every day, and match each result to the right pet."
