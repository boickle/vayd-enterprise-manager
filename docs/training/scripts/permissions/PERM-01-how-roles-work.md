# PERM-01. How roles work in Scout

- **Audience:** managers (practice admins and practice managers).
- **Length:** 2 to 3 minutes.
- **Watch first:** none. Watch this before the other PERM and ROLE videos.
- **The one thing to remember:** what a person can do is their roles plus anything granted to them individually. Nothing else gives access.

## Why (opening)

"Every screen and button in Scout checks what the person is allowed to do. Here's where that comes from, so you can set it up right the first time."

## What else you can do here

- Give someone a role: PERM-02.
- Set a license: PERM-03.
- Grant one extra permission with an end date: PERM-06.
- Change what a role can do: PERM-07.
- See every change: PERM-05.

## What other roles see

- **Practice managers** see the same Access tab, but the **License** and **Individual permissions** controls are greyed out for them. Only practice admins (and, for licenses, the medical director) can change those.
- **Everyone else** has no Settings → Staff.

## Closing

"The most common mistake is making someone an admin to fix one missing button. Give the right role, or grant that one permission. Remember: what a person can do is their roles plus their individual grants. Nothing else gives access."
