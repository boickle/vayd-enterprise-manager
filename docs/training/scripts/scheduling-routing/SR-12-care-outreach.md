# SR-12. Care outreach: pets due with nothing booked

- **Audience:** CLs.
- **Length:** 2 to 3 minutes.
- **Watch first:** SR-08 and SR-09.
- **The one thing to remember:** a pet leaves this list only when a visit is booked. Log every contact so nobody calls the family twice.

## Why (opening)

"These pets are due for care, like vaccines or an annual exam, and have nothing booked. Every one we book is care that would otherwise slip."

## What else you can do here

- **Hide** a reminder that doesn't apply (for example, given elsewhere). **Show hidden reminders** brings them back.
- **Provider** filter and **Choose Date Range**.
- **Email history** and **Messages history** per family.

## What other roles see

- **Lead techs, techs, lead CLs and managers** can work the list too.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: calling a family someone already called today, because the contact wasn't logged, and hiding a reminder instead of booking it. Remember: a pet leaves this list only when a visit is booked. Log every contact so nobody calls the family twice."
