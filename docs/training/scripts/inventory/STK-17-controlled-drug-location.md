# STK-17. Set a doctor's controlled drug location

- **Audience:** admin logins (the only people who can open this Settings screen).
- **Length:** 1 to 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** STK-05.
- **The one thing to remember:** a doctor can't be charged a controlled drug until their controlled drug location is set.

## Why (opening)

"Each doctor's controlled drugs live in their own safe. Scout needs to know which shelf that is, so checkout takes doses from it and the Controlled Drugs report checks against it."

## Steps

Signed in with an admin login.

| Step | Screen | Action | Narration | Demo data needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Settings | Click **Staff** in the Settings menu | "Open Settings, Staff." | Admin login |
| 2 | Staff | Choose **Avery Lane · Provider** in **Staff** | "Pick the doctor." | Dr. Lane marked licensed veterinarian |
| 3 | Profile | Point at **Controlled drug location** and its text: "Where this doctor's controlled drugs live (their safe)…" | "This is where her controlled drugs live." | — |
| 4 | Profile | Point at the one thing to remember (voice only) | "Remember: no location, no controlled charges." | — |
| 5 | Profile | Open **Location**: "Not set — can't charge controlled drugs", "Dr. Lane home → Controlled safe", … | "Pick her safe." | Controlled drug location cleared in the setup, so the video sets it |
| 6 | Profile | Choose **Dr. Lane home → Controlled safe** | "Dr. Lane home, Controlled safe." | — |
| 7 | Profile | Click **Save profile** | "Save." | — |
| 8 | Voice-over | — | "Her controlled doses now come from that safe at checkout." | — |

## What else you can do here

- **Default office** and **Default location**: the car checkout uses for this person.

## What other roles see

- Practice managers don't get the full Settings screen, so they can't change this (question Q2).
- The field appears only for a veterinarian marked licensed on the **Access** tab. For anyone else Scout says so.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes are forgetting it for a new doctor, and pointing it at a car instead of the safe. Remember: no location, no controlled charges."
