# STK-10. Waste a controlled drug

- **Audience:** doctors (and technicians who handle controlled drugs).
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** STK-09, STK-05.
- **The one thing to remember:** controlled waste always needs a disposal method, and the witness is whoever actually watched.

## Why (opening)

"Controlled waste is the entry an auditor asks about first. Scout's log is matched against Vet Snap, so every wasted mL needs a reason, a disposal method, and ideally a witness."

## Steps

Signed in as Dr. Avery Lane. A cracked bottle: 0.5 mL of Buprenorphine lost from lot BU-1102 in her Controlled safe.

## What else you can do here

- A dose drawn up and not given is recorded on the visit's checkout line instead (STK-05).

## What other roles see

- The Witness list is the same for everyone and leaves out whoever is signed in.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes are skipping the disposal method, and naming a witness who wasn't there. Remember: disposal method always, and a real witness."
