# ROLE-04. What a Technician and a Lead Technician see

- **Audience:** managers.
- **Length:** 2 to 3 minutes.
- **Watch first:** PERM-02.
- **The one thing to remember:** a technician works their office's visits and stock. A lead technician also moves visits for the team, moves and wastes stock, and sees inventory and appointment reports.

## Why (opening)

"Techs do the hands-on work, and a lead tech keeps the team and the stock running. Here's where their Scout differs."

## What they can't do

- **Sign a SOAP or approve a prescription:** that needs a veterinary license.
- **Marketing:** "You do not have permission to: See Marketing: email campaigns and promotions".
- **Void or refund a payment.**

## Notes to check before recording

- Several lead-tech extras (edit client details, reassign tasks, close an invoice) are marked **Not enforced yet**: both roles can do them today. Don't say a plain tech can't.

## Closing

"The most common mistake is giving a tech the Technician role without an office, so they get no stock rights or tasks. Remember: a tech works their office's visits and stock; a lead tech also runs the team's visits and stock."
