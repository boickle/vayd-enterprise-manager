## What else you can do here

- A pet who will be over the plan's age at the anniversary shows "… will be old enough for …, so we will move" and
  renews on the next plan (Puppy/Kitten → Foundations, Foundations → Golden where set).
- A member still billed through Square sees "We're moving to a new payment system" and adds a card for Stripe.
- A link opened after renewal shows "This membership already renewed".

## What other roles see

- Staff see what the owner chose on the pet's Membership panel and audit trail. The renewal job runs at 9:00 each day.

## Closing

"Pets who age out move to the next plan automatically, and the page says so. The most common mistake is telling a family their change starts today. Remember: changes and cancellations take effect at the anniversary, not now."
