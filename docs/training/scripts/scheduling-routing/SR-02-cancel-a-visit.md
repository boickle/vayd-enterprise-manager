# SR-02. Cancel a visit: Remove, with a reason

- **Audience:** everyone.
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** SR-01.
- **The one thing to remember:** every cancel needs a real reason, and a visit that has started can't be removed.

## Why (opening)

"When a visit is cancelled, the reason is kept on the visit and counted in our cancellation reports. A real reason tells us why families cancel."

## What else you can do here

- **Reschedule** instead of removing, when the family still wants a visit (SR-05).
- **Add to waitlist** before you remove, if they want the next opening (SR-09).
- When a pet has died, use the pet's wrap-up instead (SR-17). It closes the pet's visits and everything else that's open for them.

## What other roles see

- **CLs, lead CLs and managers** can remove any visit.
- **Doctors and techs** can remove any visit too (owner, Nov 7, 2026).
- **Observers** can't remove visits.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: a reason like 'cancel' that tells nobody anything, and removing a visit to start over when Reschedule would keep its history. Remember: every cancel needs a real reason, and a visit that has started can't be removed."
