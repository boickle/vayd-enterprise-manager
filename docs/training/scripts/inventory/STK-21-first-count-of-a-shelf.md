# STK-21. First count of an uncounted shelf

- **Audience:** practice manager and inventory managers.
- **Length:** 2 to 3 minutes.
- **Watch first:** STK-11, STK-16.
- **The one thing to remember:** a first count sets the shelf's starting balance. It isn't shrink. Write a count, or 0, on every lot row.

## Why (opening)

"At go-live most shelves have never been counted. Their numbers are guesses, and the Transfer and Order Lists ignore them. Counting a shelf once makes it real."

## Steps

Signed in as Robin Hale. Dr. Shah's Car 2 has never been counted; it holds 25 Carprofen caplets from lot CP-4520.

## What else you can do here

- **Upload filled sheet…** after counting on the Excel sheet.
- **When the count was taken** if you counted earlier.
- **Annual count** tab for the yearly count.

## What other roles see

- Inventory managers count their own office; doctors their own safe (STK-16).

## Closing

"The most common mistakes are leaving a lot row blank when the lot is gone, and typing last year's numbers. Remember: a first count sets the starting balance, so every lot gets a number."
