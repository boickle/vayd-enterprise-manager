# STK-15. The Vet Snap patient file every Thursday

- **Audience:** doctors and the practice manager.
- **Length:** 1 to 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** STK-14.
- **The one thing to remember:** it's one combined file for all doctors. Download it and upload it to Vet Snap every week.

## Why (opening)

"Vet Snap patient records are shared across doctors, so Vet Snap needs every client and pet who got a controlled drug. Scout builds that file for you."

## What else you can do here

- The same button is on **Analytics & Reports → Inventory → Controlled Drugs**.

## What other roles see

- Every doctor and the practice manager download the same combined file.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes are uploading only your own patients, and skipping a week. Remember: one combined file, every week."
