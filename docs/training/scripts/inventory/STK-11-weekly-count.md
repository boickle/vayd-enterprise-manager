# STK-11. Weekly count

- **Audience:** technicians (doctors when they cover).
- **Length:** 3 minutes.
- **Watch first:** STK-01.
- **The one thing to remember:** count what you see, and enter 0 rather than leaving a box blank.

## Why (opening)

"Each week Scout picks a short list of items to count at your office. Your count corrects what's on hand, and that's what the Transfer List, the orders and the shrink report all work from."

## Steps

Signed in as Jordan Pike. Counting Metronidazole 250 mg tablets: 38 left of lot MT-5001 and 60 of MT-5120 on Main, none on the cars.

## What else you can do here

- **Add lot at Main** (or a car) for a bottle that isn't listed.
- **Submit ready items** posts everything that's complete at once.
- **Full count list** counts every item at the office.

## What other roles see

- Doctors use the same screen when they cover.
- The practice manager sees the results in **Count Report** and **Shrink & Accuracy** (STK-22).

## Closing

"The most common mistakes are counting from memory, and leaving a location blank instead of 0. Remember: count what you see, and enter 0."
