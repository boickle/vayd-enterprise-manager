# LV-07. When a tech releases a result

- **Audience:** techs.
- **Length:** 1½ to 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** LV-06.
- **The one thing to remember:** only mark a result reviewed when a vet has told you to, and pick the reason that says how.

## Why (opening)

"Sometimes the doctor is in a visit and asks you to release a result. Scout lets you, and records who and why."

## What else you can do here

- Approving a client summary also asks a tech for a reason.

## What other roles see

- Doctors aren't asked for a reason.
- Client liaisons can't mark results reviewed.

## Closing

"The most common mistake: marking results reviewed to clear the list. Remember: only mark a result reviewed when a vet has told you to, and pick the reason that says how."
