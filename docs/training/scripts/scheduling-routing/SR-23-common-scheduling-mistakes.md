# SR-23. The most common scheduling mistakes

- **Audience:** everyone.
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** the rest of the series.
- **The one thing to remember:** let routing book, give the family the window, and close every loop: requests, follow-ups, holds and offers.

## Why (opening)

"Most scheduling problems come from a few habits. Here they are, and how to avoid each."
