# MEM-01. Is this pet a member? Reading the Membership section

- **Audience:** everyone (CLs, techs, doctors, managers). Filmed as Casey Morgan (client liaison).
- **Length:** about 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** nothing; this is the first membership video.
- **The one thing to remember:** the Membership section on the pet's chart is the answer. Check it before you quote a price.

## Why (opening)

"Before you quote a price or book a wellness visit, check whether the pet is a member. Members get covered services and member prices."

## What else you can do here

- **Benefit details** (under the lists) opens every benefit with its coverage type (included, copay or percent off).
- The heart also shows on the scheduler, Doctor Day, My Week and routing, but the chart is the place to check.
- Coverage on today's bill is worked out for you (MEM-08). You never key member prices by hand.

## What other roles see

- **Lead CLs and managers** also see the **Stripe subscription** row (with a link to Stripe), **Cancel**, **Add benefit** and **Benefit details & edits**.
- **Doctors, techs and CLs** see **Change plan** greyed out after the first month of the membership year, with the hover note "More than a month into this membership year. Ask a lead client liaison, practice manager or admin to change the plan."
- Badges that can appear beside **Active**: **Benefits paused** (MEM-17), **Owner changed** (MEM-16), **Inactive** or **Expired**.

## Closing

"The most common mistake is quoting from memory: what's left changes after every visit. Remember: the Membership section on the pet's chart is the answer."
