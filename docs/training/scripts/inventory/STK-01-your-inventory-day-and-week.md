# STK-01. Your inventory day and week

- **Audience:** technicians and doctors.
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** SR-15 (your day and week on the calendar).
- **The one thing to remember:** stock only changes when someone records it: Unbox, checkout, Move Items, Waste / Adjust or a count.

## Why (opening)

"Scout knows what's on your car only from what we record. If a bottle moves and nobody records it, the counts, the Transfer List and the orders are all wrong."

## What else you can do here

- **Order List** (read only unless you're the office's inventory manager).
- On a phone, Unbox, Move Items and the Transfer List open without the Pharmacy menus around them.
- The **Expiring** report is under Analytics & Reports → Inventory (STK-12).

## What other roles see

- Doctors also see **Setup → First / Annual Counts** (STK-16).
- Client liaisons see only **Order List** under Move / Order (owner, Nov 9: they don't count or unbox). Pharmacy opens on the Order List for them.
- The practice manager also sees **Store**, **Mail Order Queue**, **Auto-ships**, **Manage** and **Setup**.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes are moving stock without recording it, and logging waste days later from memory. Remember: stock only changes when someone records it."
