# STK-13. A return that can't go back on the shelf, and controlled returns

- **Audience:** client liaisons, technicians and doctors (everyone who can return items).
- **Length:** 2 to 3 minutes.
- **Watch first:** INV-11.
- **The one thing to remember:** answer "Back on the shelf?" honestly. If it can't be resold, it's recorded as waste, and a controlled drug never goes back.

## Why (opening)

"An opened bag or a bottle that's been in someone's home can't be sold again. Scout records it as waste, so it doesn't sit in the count as if it were on the shelf."

## What else you can do here

- Change **Shelf** when the item goes back somewhere other than where it was sold.
- Pick a different **Reason**, such as **Damaged**.
- For a line sold from two bottles, choose which bottle it goes back to.

## What other roles see

- Every role except observers has **Return items** (INV-11).
- A bill with a return can't be voided, unlocked or reopened ("This invoice has returns. Make a correction instead.").

## Closing

"The most common mistakes are putting an opened bag back on the shelf, and trying to restock a controlled drug. Remember: be honest about Back on the shelf, and a controlled drug is always waste."
