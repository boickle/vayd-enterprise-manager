# PRC-08. Tagalongs and reminder rules

- **Audience:** practice manager; doctors and lead client liaisons for reminder rules.
- **Length:** 2 to 3 minutes.
- **Watch first:** PRC-02.
- **The one thing to remember:** a reminder rule decides which reminder a charge creates and clears. A tagalong adds another line automatically.

## Why (opening)

"When a rabies vaccine is given, the family should get a reminder when it's due again, and the old reminder should clear. That's set once, on the item."

## What else you can do here

- **Stock item, Draws from**: which stock item this one counts against.
- Tagalong options: quantity range, how many to add, tax before or after.

## What other roles see

- Doctors and lead client liaisons can change reminder rules; tagalongs are for managers.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes are a rule that creates a reminder but doesn't clear the old one, and a tagalong that charges without a discount. Remember: the reminder rule decides what's created and cleared."
