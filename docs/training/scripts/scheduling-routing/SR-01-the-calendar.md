# SR-01. Find your way around the calendar

- **Audience:** everyone (CLs, techs, doctors, managers).
- **Length:** 2 to 3 minutes.
- **Watch first:** none.
- **The one thing to remember:** right-click a visit to see everything you can do with it.

## Why (opening)

"Every scheduling job starts on this calendar. Once you know where things are, the rest of the series is easy."

## What else you can do here

- **GO TO DATE** jumps to any date.
- **APPOINTMENT TYPE** filters the calendar to one kind of visit.
- **Work Zones Map** shows the zones.
- **Optimize** has its own video (SR-14), and **Fill** on each day is the Schedule loader (SR-13).
- **Maps** and the printer icon give the day's route and a printable My Day.
- **Scheduling → Appointment Search** finds a client's or pet's visits by name.

## What other roles see

- **Doctors, techs and CLs** see the same calendar and the same right-click menu.
- **Techs** also see **Create deposit** in the left menu (for cash and checks they bring back).
- **Observers** can look but can't change anything.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: reading the visit's start time as the arrival time (the family was promised the window), and searching menus for an action that's on the right-click menu. Remember: right-click a visit to see everything you can do with it."
