# SR-18. Staff hours, zones and days off

- **Audience:** managers (admin logins; see the notes).
- **Length:** 2 to 3 minutes.
- **Watch first:** SR-01.
- **The one thing to remember:** hours and zones decide what routing and online booking offer. Change them here first, before anyone books around a change.

## Why (opening)

"Routing and online booking only offer times inside each doctor's hours and zones. If those are wrong, families get offered times nobody can keep."

## What else you can do here

- **Work Zones Map** to see the zones.
- **Appointment types** tab: which visit types this doctor can do, and which are bookable online.
- **Goals** and **Access** tabs.

## What other roles see

- **Only admin logins see this page.** A practice manager without an admin login sees only **Settings: Memberships**, although the permission list gives managers "Staff schedules, zones, depots and offices" (see the review, Q12).

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: booking around a doctor's day off without marking it here, so online booking keeps offering it, and changing a zone without checking that day's visits. Remember: hours and zones decide what routing and online booking offer. Change them here first."
