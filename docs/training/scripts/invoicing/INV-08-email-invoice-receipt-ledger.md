# INV-08. Email an invoice, receipt or household ledger

- **Audience:** CLs. Other staff can watch too.
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** EMR V5.
- **The one thing to remember:** the same button sends an invoice before payment and a receipt after.

## Why (opening)

"Families ask for receipts for insurance, reimbursement and their own records. It takes one click."

## Steps

| Step | Screen | Action | Narration | Demo data needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Clients, Maria Rivera, Ledger | Click today's paid invoice | "Open the bill on Maria's ledger." | Paid Rivera invoice. Casey Morgan's login needs access to the Info or Field shared inbox |
| 2 | Invoice | Point at the one thing to remember | "One button sends an invoice before payment, and a receipt after." | — |
| 3 | Invoice | Point at **Receipt · Paid** and **Email receipt** | "This bill is paid, so the button says Email receipt." | — |
| 4 | Invoice | Click **Email receipt** | "Click it." | — |
| 5 | **Email receipt** window | Point at "Review and edit the email before sending to Maria Rivera." | "Check the address and the message." | — |
| 6 | **Email receipt** window | Click **Send email** | "Send it." | — |
| 7 | An open Rivera invoice | Point at **Email invoice** | "On an unpaid bill, the same button says Email invoice." | Open Rivera invoice |
| 8 | Ledger | Point at **Email Ledger**: "Emails the household ledger. Deleted and voided items stay off the client copy." | "For a statement of the whole household, use Email Ledger." | — |
| 9 | Invoice | Point at **Print** | "Print gives a paper copy." | — |

## What else you can do here

- **Email pay link** and **Text pay link** (EMR V15).
- **Return items** (INV-11).

## What other roles see

- Every visit role and billing can email invoices, receipts and statements, as long as their login has a shared inbox.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: sending to an old email address, and sending an invoice when they wanted a receipt. Pay first, then send. Remember: the same button sends an invoice before payment and a receipt after."
