# EMR V7 (re-recorded). Patient alerts

- **Audience:** everyone who opens a SOAP: doctors, techs, CLs.
- **Length:** 1 to 1½ minutes.
- **Watch first:** none.
- **The one thing to remember:** read the alert banner before you touch the pet, and add one when there's something the next person must know.

Replaces the first half of "EMR V7 – Patient alerts and in-house labs" (owner, Nov 8, 2026). The lab half is now LV-01 and LV-02.

## Why (opening)

"An alert is the one thing about a pet everyone must know before they walk in: a bite risk, a drug reaction, a fearful cat."
