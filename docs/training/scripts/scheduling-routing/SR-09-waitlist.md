# SR-09. The waitlist: add a family, then fill an opening

- **Audience:** CLs; doctors and techs can add families too.
- **Length:** 3 minutes.
- **Watch first:** SR-03.
- **The one thing to remember:** when a visit cancels, check the waitlist before the slot goes to waste.

## Why (opening)

"Cancellations happen every day. The waitlist turns a lost hour into a visit for a family who wanted to come in sooner."

## What else you can do here

- **Text client** and **Messages** on each card.
- **Edit** and **Remove** a card. Tabs: **Waiting**, **Booked**, **Removed**.
- **Put on waitlist for a sooner appointment** in the Book window (SR-04) adds a family while you book.
- **+ Add to waitlist** at the top of the page, for a family with no visit yet.

## What other roles see

- **Doctors, techs, CLs and managers** can add families and fill openings.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: letting a cancelled slot sit empty, and adding a family without saying how soon they can come. Remember: when a visit cancels, check the waitlist before the slot goes to waste."
