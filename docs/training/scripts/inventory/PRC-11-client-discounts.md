# PRC-11. Client discount statuses and staff pricing

- **Audience:** admin logins.
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** PRC-06, INV-15.
- **The one thing to remember:** a status gives a standing discount to every client who has it. Items marked "Exclude percentage discounts" skip it, except for staff.

## Why (opening)

"Seniors, staff, family and friends each get a standing discount. It's set once here and applied to every bill, estimate and Room Loader."

## Steps

Signed in with an admin login.

| Step | Screen | Action | Narration | Demo data needed |

| 2 | Client discounts | Point at the table: Employee Discount (EMP), "Inv cost+10% · Proc 20% · Lab 20%", Staff Yes; Senior Discount 10% (SR10), 10% | "Each status has its discount. Employees pay cost plus 10% on products." | Statuses Employee Discount and Senior Discount 10% |

## What else you can do here

- **Inactivate** a status to hide it from new picks.

## What other roles see

- Only admin logins open the full Settings.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes are creating a one-off status instead of a manual discount, and forgetting excluded items. Remember: a status discounts every bill for that client."
