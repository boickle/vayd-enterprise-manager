# ROLE-08. What Billing / AR sees

- **Audience:** managers.
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** ROLE-05.
- **The one thing to remember:** Billing / AR handles the money (payments, voids, refunds, credits and deposits) without clinical access.

## Why (opening)

"Billing / AR chases balances and fixes payments. They need the money tools, not the medical records."

## Steps

Logged in as **Pat Lowe** (Billing / AR).

| Step | Screen | Action | Narration | Demo data needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Home | Point at the top bar: **Home, Scheduling, Catalog, Pharmacy, Labs, Tasks** and **Create deposit** in the left panel | "This is Pat, in Billing. No Settings, but Create deposit is here." | perm-seed.ts |
| 2 | Voice-over | — | "The one thing to remember: Billing handles the money without clinical access." | — |
| 3 | Clients → Tom Chen, financial tab | Point at **Void** on the payment, **Void invoice** and **Add credit** | "On a bill, Pat can void a payment, void the invoice, or add credit." | Paid $75 cash bill on the Chens |
| 4 | Analytics | Point at the tabs: **Financial** (Payments, Projected Revenue, Gift Cards), **Memberships, Inventory** | "Analytics covers payments, gift cards, memberships and inventory value." | — |
| 5 | Voice-over | — | "Billing can also refund card payments, edit a posted payment, and issue gift cards. Cancelling a membership goes to a lead client liaison or a manager." (Owner, Nov 10: Billing no longer cancels memberships or overrides benefits.) | — |

## What they can't do

- **Clinical work:** no SOAPs, prescriptions or vaccine records.
- **Settings:** "Access Denied".
- **Marketing.**

## Closing

"The most common mistake is giving Billing a CL role to reach a client's record. Billing already sees clients and their bills. Remember: Billing handles the money without clinical access."
