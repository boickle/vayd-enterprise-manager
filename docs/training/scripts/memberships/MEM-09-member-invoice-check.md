# MEM-09. Check a member's invoice before checkout

- **Audience:** CLs. Filmed as Casey Morgan (client liaison) on the visit's **Checkout** page.
- **Length:** about 1¼ minutes.
- **Watch first:** INV-02, MEM-08.
- **The one thing to remember:** covered lines say covered, and a member never gets two discounts on one line.

## Why (opening)

"Before you take payment from a member, take ten seconds to check the bill. Covered services should be free, and member prices applied."

## What else you can do here

- **Reprice** previews each change before applying it (INV-14).
- A pet whose benefits are paused shows **Benefits paused** on the bill: charge regular prices (MEM-17).
- Hidden lines and tax are explained in INV-02.

## What other roles see

- The same for every role that can take payment.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: typing over a member price by hand, and taking payment before repricing a brand-new member. Remember: covered lines say covered, and a member never gets two discounts on one line."
