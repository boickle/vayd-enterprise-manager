# PRC-03. Add a new item, lab or procedure

- **Audience:** practice manager.
- **Length:** 2 to 3 minutes.
- **Watch first:** PRC-02.
- **The one thing to remember:** check the tax level, and for stock, the lot rules, before you save.

## Why (opening)

"A new test or product has to be in Catalog before anyone can charge for it. Getting the tax and lot settings right the first time saves fixing bills later."

## Steps

Signed in as Robin Hale. Adding a new in-house lab, Fecal float, at $35.

## What else you can do here

- **Product** has six steps: Details, Clinical, Lots, Stock, Pricing, Associations.
- **Exclude percentage discounts** on Details (PRC-06).
- **Pricing** and **Associations** (price breaks, reminders, tagalongs) later (PRC-04, PRC-08).

## What other roles see

- Only people who can edit the catalog see **Add item**.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes are taxing a lab, and adding a vaccine without requiring a lot number. Remember: check the tax level, and for stock, the lot rules."
