# SR-11. The Holds board

- **Audience:** CLs.
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** SR-04.
- **The one thing to remember:** a hold isn't a visit. No reminder goes out until you turn it into a real visit type, or release it.

## Why (opening)

"Holds keep a time for a family who hasn't confirmed. Left alone, they block the doctor's day, and the family never gets a reminder."

## Steps

| Step | Screen | Action | Narration | Demo data needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Scheduling → Holds** | Point at "Calendar holds scheduled today (Eastern time) and later…" | "Every hold on the calendar is listed here." | A HOLD for Biscuit Chen with Dr. Lane, "Hold: new kitten exam, owner to confirm", placed by Casey. Signed in as Casey Morgan (CL) |
| 2 | Holds | Point at the one thing to remember | "A hold isn't a visit. No reminder goes out until you turn it into a real visit type, or release it." | — |
| 3 | Holds | Click **Mine**, then **All** | "See your holds, unassigned ones, or all of them." | — |
| 4 | Card | Point at the time, **Provider: Avery Lane**, the note, **Manual**, **Within 3 business days**, **By Casey Morgan** | "Each card shows when, with whom, why, and who's following up." | — |
| 5 | Card | Point at **Call**, **Text client**, **Email client** and the **Contact log** | "Reach the family from here, and log it." | — |
| 6 | Card | Point at **Assign to…** | "Hand it to a colleague if needed." | — |
| 7 | Card | Click **Open / convert** | "When the family confirms, Open / convert takes you to the hold on the calendar." | — |
| 8 | Calendar | Right-click the hold, **Scheduling → Edit Appointment**, change **Type** from HOLD to **Wellness**, then save | "Change the type to the real visit. Now it's a visit, with reminders." | — |
| 9 | Voice-over | — | "If the family says no, Remove hold on the card frees the time." | — |

## What else you can do here

- **Reschedule** moves the hold.
- **Email history** and **Messages history**.
- **Unassign me**.

## What other roles see

- **Techs, lead techs, lead CLs and managers** can work the board too.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: leaving a hold on the calendar after the family confirms, so they get no reminder, and holds nobody follows up. Remember: a hold isn't a visit. No reminder goes out until you turn it into a real visit type, or release it."
