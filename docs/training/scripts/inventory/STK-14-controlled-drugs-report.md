# STK-14. Controlled Drugs report: match your doses to Vet Snap

- **Audience:** doctors; also the Controlled Substances Director and the practice manager.
- **Length:** 2 to 3 minutes.
- **Watch first:** STK-05.
- **The one thing to remember:** check that every dose under your name is yours and came from your safe, then tick **Reconciled**.

## Why (opening)

"Your Vet Snap log and Scout should tell the same story. This report lists every controlled dose, return and waste under your name, so you can match them line by line."

## Steps

Signed in as Dr. Avery Lane. Biscuit Chen got 0.6 mL of Buprenorphine from her Controlled safe today, and Maple Rivera got 0.4 mL charged under her name from the Raleigh office car (lot BU-0990).

## What else you can do here

- **Download CSV** and **Print log**.
- **Select all to reconcile** when everything matches.
- **Vet Snap patients (last 14 days)** (STK-15).

## What other roles see

- The practice manager and the Controlled Substances Director pick any **Doctor** (or All doctors).
- Technicians don't see this report.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes are ticking Reconciled without opening Vet Snap, and ignoring a dose from someone else's shelf. Remember: check each dose is yours, then tick Reconciled."
