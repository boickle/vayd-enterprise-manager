# PRC-06. Hide on invoice, Exclude percentage discounts and tax level

- **Audience:** practice manager.
- **Length:** 2 to 3 minutes.
- **Watch first:** PRC-02, INV-02.
- **The one thing to remember:** a line with a charge always shows to the client. "Hide on invoice" only hides $0 lines.

## Why (opening)

"Three settings on each item change what the client sees and pays: hide on invoice, exclude percentage discounts, and the tax level. Each one is easy to set by accident."
