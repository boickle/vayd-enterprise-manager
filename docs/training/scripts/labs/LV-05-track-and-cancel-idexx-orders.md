# LV-05. Track and cancel IDEXX orders

- **Audience:** techs.
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** LV-03.
- **The one thing to remember:** to drop an IDEXX test, remove its charge from the bill, and Scout cancels the order. If you cancel from this page instead, remove the charge too.

## Why (opening)

"Labs → IDEXX results shows every order from Scout, from made to final. Check it daily so no sample is stuck."

## What else you can do here

- **All providers**: pick one doctor.
- **Finish in IDEXX** on a row reopens IDEXX's page.
- **To review** and **Unmatched**: LV-06 and LV-09.

## What other roles see

- Doctors see the same. Once IDEXX has the sample, Scout can't cancel it; call IDEXX.

## Closing

"The most common mistake: cancelling the order here and leaving the charge on the bill. Remember: to drop an IDEXX test, remove its charge from the bill, and Scout cancels the order."
