# MEM-20. What members see in the portal

> **Dry run:** passed Nov 9, 2026 (`mem-20-portal.cy.ts`). Changes: the button is **What's included & what's left** (not View benefits); the savings page (**See my membership savings**) is for pets that aren't members and shows what a membership would cover, so it's filmed on Juniper; the benefits window also holds the card change.

- **Audience:** CLs. Filmed on Maria's portal login.
- **Length:** about 1½ minutes.
- **Watch first:** MEM-06, MEM-10.
- **The one thing to remember:** everything about a member pet is on that pet's card in the portal: benefits,
  savings and renewal.

## Why (opening)

"Members ask what's included, what's left, and how much they've saved. They can see all of it in the portal, so you can walk them to it."

## What else you can do here

- The owner can change the card the membership is billed on from the portal.
- **Sign up** on a non-member pet's card opens the signup page (MEM-06).

## What other roles see

- Staff see the same information on the pet's chart (MEM-01, MEM-10).

## Closing

"The most common mistake is reading the plan template instead of the pet's own benefits. Remember: everything about a member pet is on that pet's card in the portal: benefits, savings and renewal."
