# LV-12. Lab history on the chart, including eVet

- **Audience:** doctors and techs.
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** none.
- **The one thing to remember:** every lab, from Scout, IDEXX and eVet, is on the chart's **Labs** tab. eVet ones are read-only.

## Why (opening)

"Past results matter: last year's 4Dx, a glucose trend. Scout keeps them all in one place, including what was done in eVet."

## What else you can do here

- **History**: who entered, reviewed or approved each one (LV-07).
- **Row details** for a denser list.

## What other roles see

- Same for all staff.

## Closing

"The most common mistake: thinking old eVet labs are gone. Remember: every lab, from Scout, IDEXX and eVet, is on the Labs tab."
