# SR-25. The Records list: chase what's late

- **Audience:** CLs.
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** SR-24.
- **The one thing to remember:** a red "visit is close" row means call or resend today. What matters is how close the visit is, not how long we've waited.

## Why (opening)

"Records that arrive the day after the visit don't help. This list shows which requests are about to be too late."

## What else you can do here

- **Gmail** searches the shared inbox for this patient's records.
- **Still waiting**, **Received**, **Closed out** and **All** filters.

## What other roles see

- **Every scheduling role** can work the list.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: sorting by how long ago we asked instead of how close the visit is, and marking received without filing the document. Remember: a red visit-is-close row means call or resend today."
