# STK-12. Expiring report and expired bottles

- **Audience:** technicians and doctors.
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** STK-09.
- **The one thing to remember:** anything under "Expired on shelf" can't be used. Take it off the shelf and log it on Waste / Adjust as Expired.

## Why (opening)

"An expired bottle can't be charged, used at a visit or mailed. Scout blocks it. So the sooner it's off the shelf and logged, the cleaner your car and your counts are."
