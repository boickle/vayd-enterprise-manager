# LV-10. IDEXX lab charges on the bill

- **Audience:** client liaisons.
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** none.
- **The one thing to remember:** tests the doctor adds in IDEXX are charged by Scout. If one can't be, Billing gets a task. Don't add it by hand.

## Why (opening)

"IDEXX tests are on the bill like any other charge. What's different is that the doctor can add tests inside IDEXX, and Scout keeps the bill in step."

## What else you can do here

- **Send to IDEXX** on an unsent IDEXX line (LV-03).

## What other roles see

- Techs and doctors see the same bill.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: adding an IDEXX add-on by hand, so it's charged twice, and ignoring Billing tasks. Remember: tests added in IDEXX are charged by Scout; if one can't be, Billing gets a task."
