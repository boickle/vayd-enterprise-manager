# STK-25. Settings: waste reasons, disposal methods, count alerts

- **Audience:** admin logins.
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** STK-09, STK-22.
- **The one thing to remember:** these settings decide what staff must enter when they waste stock, and when you get alerted.

## Why (opening)

"The waste reasons, disposal rules and alert limits are set once for the whole practice. Getting them right means the reports flag what matters and nothing else."

## Steps

Signed in with an admin login.

| Step | Screen | Action | Narration | Demo data needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Settings | Click **Inventory**, then **Waste / disposal** | "Open Waste and disposal." | Admin login |
| 2 | Waste + disposal | Point at the reasons: "Expired — disposal: yes", "Damaged — disposal: optional", … | "Each reason says whether a disposal method is needed." | Practice waste reasons |
| 3 | Waste + disposal | Point at the one thing to remember (voice only) | "Remember: these decide what staff must enter." | — |
| 4 | Waste + disposal | Point at "Drawn up, not given — Used by checkout" and "Returned, not resellable — Used by returns" | "Two reasons are used by checkout and returns, so they can't be archived." | — |
| 5 | Waste + disposal | Point at **Disposal methods**, including "Reverse Distribution (Controlled Drugs Only)" | "The disposal methods staff pick from." | — |
| 6 | Waste + disposal | Point at **Require disposal method for**: Prescription medications, Vaccines, Refrigerated items | "Which items always need a disposal method. Controlled drugs always do." | — |
| 7 | Waste + disposal | Point at **Alert if single waste > $** and **Monthly waste > $** | "When a waste is big enough, the manager gets a task." | — |
| 8 | Settings | Click **Count alerts** | "Count alerts." | — |
| 9 | Count alerts | Point at **Monthly shrink review**: Shrink over %, Count accuracy under % | "Limits for the monthly shrink review." | — |
| 10 | Count alerts | Point at **Stale lots review** thresholds | "And what counts as a stale lot." | — |

## What else you can do here

- **+ Add reason**, **+ Add disposal method**, **Archive**.
- **Require notes for reasons**.

## What other roles see

- Only admin logins open the full Settings (question Q2).

## Closing

"The most common mistakes are making every reason require notes, so staff stop logging waste, and setting alert limits so low that alerts get ignored. Remember: these settings decide what staff must enter."
