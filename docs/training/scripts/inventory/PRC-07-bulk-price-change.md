# PRC-07. Bulk price change

- **Audience:** practice manager.
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** PRC-02.
- **The one thing to remember:** check the item count and give a reason before you apply. Bulk changes the price, not the markup.

## Why (opening)

"When a whole group of prices goes up, changing them one at a time takes hours. Bulk does it in one step, with a record of who did it and why."

## Steps

Signed in as Robin Hale. Raising Cerenia by 5%.

| Step | Screen | Action | Narration | Demo data needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Catalog | Click **Select inventory rows for bulk price change** | "Turn on bulk selection." | — |
| 2 | Catalog | Type `Cerenia`, tick the row | "Tick the items." | — |
| 3 | Catalog | Click **Bulk adjust prices…** | "Bulk adjust prices." | — |
| 4 | Bulk price adjustment | Point at "Applies to 1 inventory catalog item(s)." | "Check how many items it applies to." | — |
| 5 | Bulk price adjustment | Point at the one thing to remember (voice only) | "Remember: check the count, and give a reason." | — |
| 6 | Bulk price adjustment | Type `5` in **% change — practice list / in-clinic price** | "Five percent up. A negative number lowers prices." | — |
| 7 | Bulk price adjustment | Type `Supplier increase Nov 2026` in **Reason (required to apply)** | "The reason is required." | — |
| 8 | Bulk price adjustment | Click **Apply to selected** | "Apply." | — |
| 9 | Voice-over | — | "Bulk changes the price, not the markup, so the next cost change can recalculate from the old markup. Check marked-up items afterwards." | — |

## What else you can do here

- **% change — online store price**, **Flat add ($)** for either.
- **Clear selection**.

## What other roles see

- Only the practice manager.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes are running it twice, and forgetting the store price is separate. Remember: check the count, and give a reason."
