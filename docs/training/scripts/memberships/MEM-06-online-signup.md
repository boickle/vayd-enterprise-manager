# MEM-06. Walk a client through online signup in the portal

- **Audience:** CLs (what the client sees, so they can help on the phone). Filmed on Maria's portal login, then
  Juniper's chart as Casey Morgan (CL).
- **Length:** about 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** MEM-01.
- **The one thing to remember:** clients pick the plan and pay for it themselves. Your job is to guide them.

## Why (opening)

"Many families join online, from the client portal. When they call with questions, it helps to know exactly what they see."

## What else you can do here

- **Have a promo code?** on the payment page takes a discount code (MEM-23).
- A pet with no booked visit gets "Schedule an Appointment First" instead of the plans. That's the most common
  "my pet isn't there" call.
- A puppy or kitten under 1½ years whose owner answers "No" to more than one round of core vaccines is also offered
  the **Puppy / Kitten Add-on**.
- Pets already on a plan don't appear in the pet list.

## What other roles see

- Staff can open this page too, but should only ever walk the client through it on the client's own login.

## Closing

"The most common question: Pet not listed? Check that the pet has a booked appointment. Remember: clients pick the plan and pay for it themselves. Your job is to guide them."
