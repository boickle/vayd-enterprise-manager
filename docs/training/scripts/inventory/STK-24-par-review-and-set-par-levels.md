# STK-24. Par Review and Set par levels

- **Audience:** practice manager.
- **Length:** 2 to 3 minutes.
- **Watch first:** STK-20.
- **The one thing to remember:** Par Review suggests targets from real use, and nothing changes until you accept.

## Why (opening)

"Usage changes with the season. Par Review compares each target with the last 90 days of use, so you don't have to."

## Steps

Signed in as Robin Hale.

| Step | Screen | Action | Narration | Demo data needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pharmacy | Click **Manage**, then **Par Review** | "Open Par Review." | Some usage in the last 90 days |
| 2 | Par review | Choose **Office**: Raleigh office | "Pick the office." | — |

| 11 | Voice-over | — | "On the first of each month you get a task when there's something to review." | — |

## What else you can do here

- **Settings** on Par Review (days of use and the 25% limit).
- **Review changes** on Set par levels before anything is saved.

## What other roles see

- Only the practice manager.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes are accepting every suggestion without a look, and setting pars on shelves that were never counted. Remember: nothing changes until you accept."
