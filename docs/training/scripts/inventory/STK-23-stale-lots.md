# STK-23. Stale lots

- **Audience:** practice manager.
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** STK-12.
- **The one thing to remember:** a stale lot is stock being skipped, stuck nearly empty, or not moving. Check it on the next count before it expires.

## Why (opening)

"Bottles that get skipped or sit untouched are money that expires on the shelf. This report finds them while there's still time."
