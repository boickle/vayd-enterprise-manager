# ROLE-06. What an Inventory Manager sees

- **Audience:** managers.
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** PERM-02.
- **The one thing to remember:** an inventory manager runs stock at the office they're assigned to: counts, par levels, orders and suppliers. Their office decides where, and who gets the automatic inventory tasks.

## Why (opening)

"Inventory managers keep the shelves and cars stocked. Their role is per office, so they only manage the stock where they work."

## What they can't do

- **Clinical records:** no SOAPs or prescriptions.
- **Another office's stock**, unless they're also ticked for that office.
- **Marketing** and payment reversals.

## Closing

"The most common mistake is forgetting the office, so nobody gets the count tasks. Remember: an inventory manager runs stock at the office they're assigned to."
