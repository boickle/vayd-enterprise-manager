# PERM-06. Give one person one extra permission, with an end date

- **Audience:** practice admins.
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** PERM-01.
- **The one thing to remember:** use a grant for one person instead of changing the role for everyone, and give it an end date when it's temporary.

## Why (opening)

"Sometimes one person needs one more thing, like a client liaison covering for her lead while she's away. A grant gives exactly that, to that person, for as long as you say."

## Steps

Logged in as **Quinn Parker** (Practice Admin). Dana Cole (Lead CL) is away for two weeks; Lou Bennett (CL) covers membership cancellations.

| Step | Screen | Action | Narration | Demo data needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Settings → Staff | Pick **Lou Bennett**, click **Access** | "Lou is covering for Dana for two weeks." | Lou Bennett: Client Liaison |
| 2 | Access | Point at **Individual permissions**: "Nothing granted beyond Lou Bennett's roles." | "Individual permissions add something beyond the role." | — |
| 3 | Voice-over | — | "The one thing to remember: grant it to one person instead of changing the role for everyone, and give it an end date." | — |
| 4 | Access | In **Permission**, pick **Memberships — Cancel a membership** | "Pick the permission: Cancel a membership." | — |
| 5 | Access | Set **Ends (optional)** to two weeks from today | "Set when it ends: the day Dana's back." | — |
| 6 | Access | Click **Grant** | "Click Grant." | — |
| 7 | Grant "Cancel a membership" | Click the preset **Covering while manager is away** | "Give the reason. It's shown in the log and on this tab." | — |
| 8 | Grant "Cancel a membership" | Click **Grant** | "Grant it." | — |
| 9 | Access | Point at the grant: "Covering while manager is away · Quinn Parker · ends …" and the **granted** tag in **What they can do today** | "It's listed with its reason and end date. It ends at the end of that day, on its own." | — |
| 10 | Access | Point at **Remove** next to the grant | "Remove takes it away early." | — |

## What else you can do here

- Executives: grant **Marketing — Create, edit and send email campaigns; edit membership and appointment request promotions** to let one executive edit campaigns and promotions (see ROLE-07).
- Some permissions can't be granted at all, because they come from a license (signing, prescribing) or belong to the platform.

## What other roles see

- **Practice managers** don't see the grant controls. Only practice admins grant permissions.
- Nobody can grant something to themselves.

## Closing

"The most common mistake is a grant with no end date for something temporary, so the access never goes away. Remember: grant it to one person instead of changing the role, and give it an end date when it's temporary."
