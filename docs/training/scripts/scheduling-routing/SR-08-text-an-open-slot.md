# SR-08. Text a family an open slot

- **Audience:** CLs and lead techs.
- **Length:** 2 to 3 minutes.
- **Watch first:** SR-09 (the offer starts from the waitlist, care outreach or the schedule loader).
- **The one thing to remember:** a text offer doesn't hold the slot. Check Texted offers before you send another.

## Why (opening)

"A text offer lets the family grab a time with one tap, without phone tag. But the time isn't theirs until they confirm."

## What else you can do here

- **Email offer** sends the same offer by email.
- **Need a different time?** on the family's page asks what works better.
- **To review**, **Expired** and **Removed** in Texted offers. Expand a row for the message and follow-up actions.
- **Use template** in the text window, for other wording.

## What other roles see

- **Lead techs, lead CLs, CLs and managers** can send offers. **Techs** can send only for their own visits.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: telling the family the time is booked before they confirm, and sending a second offer while the first is still pending. Remember: a text offer doesn't hold the slot. Check Texted offers before you send another."
